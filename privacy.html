Kitty Manager: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Kitty Manager (kitty-manager.com) helps a group run a chit or kitty fairly. It keeps records only. It never holds or moves money.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details: when you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. When you sign in with a phone number we receive that number.
  • What you enter: chit names, member names, optional phone numbers, lucky numbers, payment amounts and dates, notes, party details and bills.
  • Payment screenshots: pictures you choose to add as proof of payment.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your chits to you and to the people in your chit group, to record payments, and to keep the history of each chit. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your data for advertising or tracking.

Who can see it

Members of a chit see that chit's records. A payment screenshot can be opened only by the person who added it, the winner it was paid to, and the treasurer. Invite codes are visible only to the treasurer.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which provides sign-in and the database. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting your data

A chit's history is kept so that every member can verify the books. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time: see how to delete your account.

Children

Kitty Manager is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
