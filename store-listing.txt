APP NAME (30 max): Kitty Manager

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max):
Run your chit or kitty fairly: lottery draw, due dates and shared records.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Kitty Manager helps a group of friends, family or colleagues run a chit or kitty with trust and clarity.

What you can do
- Start a chit in a few taps: members, monthly amount, due day and grace days.
- Fair lottery draw: winners are chosen by a lottery that every member can verify.
- See who has paid on one simple board that everyone shares.
- Add payments with an optional screenshot. The winner or treasurer confirms.
- Send each member a join code on WhatsApp.
- Plan kitty parties, split bills and keep a Tea and Samosa day.
- Use it as treasurer or as member, in English, Hindi and more.

Safe and honest
- Payment screenshots are private to the person who sent it, the winner and the treasurer.
- The history only grows. Old entries cannot be edited.
- Kitty Manager keeps records only. It never holds or moves money.
